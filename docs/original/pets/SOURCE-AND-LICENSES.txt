IMPORTANT LICENSE BASIS
-----------------------
Petdex source code is MIT; pet artwork is owned by each submitter
under the license they declared. See:
  https://github.com/crafter-station/petdex/blob/main/README.md
  (“The source code is MIT. Pet assets are owned by their submitters
   under whatever license they choose to declare.”)

Public v1/v2 manifests and each pet’s pet.json / ZIP do NOT include
an SPDX license field. Pet pages’ JSON-LD also omit license.
The official search API documents this query parameter:
  ?commercial=1  — narrows results to pets whose creator declared a
  license allowing commercial use. Pets predating that field stay out.
Those commercial licenses, as defined in Petdex source
  src/lib/submissions-validation.ts  (COMMERCIAL_PET_LICENSES)
are exactly: cc0, cc-by, cc-by-sa.
Petdex also states that unspecified (pre-field) pets must not be
treated as granted permission (src/lib/pet-search.ts).

This pack includes ONLY pets that, at collection time, appeared in
https://petdex.dev/api/pets/search?commercial=1 (total 388 of 5340).
The exact SPDX among CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA is NOT published in the
public manifest, pet.json, or pet page payload, so all three official
legal texts are included below, and attribution is provided for every
pet (safe if the undeclared-to-us variant is CC BY or CC BY-SA).
If a work is CC BY-SA, share-alike obligations may apply to adaptations.
This pack does not claim a single SPDX for the combined archive.

CC licenses only cover the submitter’s own copyright in the artwork.
They cannot authorize third-party character/trademark rights. This
starter therefore prefers Petdex curated, original-looking companions
and does not include obvious fan-art of third-party characters, even
when those slugs also appear in the commercial filter.
